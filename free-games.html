Best Free Multiplayer PC Games to Play With Friends

Looking for something fun to play without spending money? There are plenty of multiplayer PC games that offer free access to their core gameplay.

From competitive shooters to sports games and cooperative experiences, these recommendations offer different ways to enjoy gaming with friends.

Some free-to-play games include optional purchases such as cosmetic items, battle passes, or additional content.

Our Favorite Free Multiplayer PC Games

1. Fortnite

Battle Royale

Fortnite is a popular multiplayer game featuring competitive battles, creative experiences, and a variety of game modes.

Players can team up with friends, compete against other squads, and explore different gameplay experiences.

Genre: Battle Royale
Platform: Epic Games Store
Best For: Competitive Squads

2. Rocket League

Sports and Racing

Rocket League combines soccer with rocket-powered vehicles to create a fast-paced multiplayer sports experience.

Friends can work together to score goals, defend their net, and improve their teamwork.

Genre: Sports
Platform: Epic Games Store
Best For: Quick Competitive Matches

3. Valorant

Tactical Shooter

Valorant is a competitive first-person shooter that combines precise gunplay with unique character abilities.

Teams must communicate, coordinate strategies, and work together to win rounds.

Genre: Tactical FPS
Platform: Riot Games Client
Best For: Strategic Teamwork

4. Apex Legends

Squad-Based Action

Apex Legends is a fast-paced battle royale game featuring characters with different abilities.

Players must combine movement, communication, and teamwork to compete against opposing squads.

Genre: Battle Royale
Platform: Steam / EA App
Best For: Action-Focused Groups

5. Counter-Strike 2

Competitive FPS

Counter-Strike 2 offers tactical multiplayer gameplay where accuracy and communication are essential.

Its free-to-play offering makes it an accessible option for players interested in competitive shooting.

Genre: Tactical FPS
Platform: Steam
Best For: Competitive Friend Groups

6. Overwatch 2

Team-Based Hero Shooter

Overwatch 2 features team-based battles where players select heroes with different abilities and roles.

Players work together to complete objectives, protect teammates, and defeat opponents.

Genre: Hero Shooter
Platform: Battle.net / Steam
Best For: Team-Oriented Players

How to Choose the Right Free Game

Before downloading a new multiplayer game, consider the interests and experience levels of your friends.

  • For competitive players: Try Valorant or Counter-Strike 2.
  • For casual competition: Try Rocket League.
  • For battle royale fans: Try Fortnite or Apex Legends.
  • For team-based objectives: Try Overwatch 2.
