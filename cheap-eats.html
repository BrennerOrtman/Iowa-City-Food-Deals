Student Budget Tip

Small changes in daily food spending can add up over a semester.

If you purchase lunch five days per week at an average cost of $8 per meal, your weekly lunch spending would be $40.

Choosing meals that cost $6 instead would reduce that spending to $30.

That's a savings of $10 per week.

Over a 16-week semester, those savings could total $160.

This is an example calculation, not a guarantee of restaurant savings.

Additional Ways Students Can Save Money

  • Compare restaurant menu prices before ordering.
  • Look for weekly food specials.
  • Take advantage of eligible student discounts.
  • Split larger meals with friends.
  • Prepare some meals at home.
  • Limit delivery fees and additional charges.
  • Set a weekly dining budget.
