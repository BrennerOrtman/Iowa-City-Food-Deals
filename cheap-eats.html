Affordable Food for College Students

Being a college student often means balancing classes, work, and a limited food budget. Fortunately, affordable meals do not have to be boring.

Our fictional Iowa City dining guide features several student-friendly meal options priced at $10 or less.

$6

Student Budget Tip

If you purchase lunch five days per week at an average cost of $8 per meal, your weekly lunch spending would be $40.

Choosing meals that cost $6 instead could reduce that spending to $30, saving approximately $10 per week.

Over a 16-week semester, those savings could total $160.

Additional Ways Students Can Save

  • Bring reusable water bottles instead of buying drinks.
  • Take advantage of restaurant lunch specials.
  • Split large pizzas or appetizers with roommates.
  • Prepare some meals at home each week.
  • Compare meal prices before deciding where to eat.
  • Use a weekly budget to monitor dining expenses.
