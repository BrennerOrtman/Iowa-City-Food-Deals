Our Story

SquadUp Gaming was imagined by three college friends: Tyler Brooks, Marcus Reed, and Jordan Ellis.

After years of playing multiplayer games together, they realized that choosing what to play was often the hardest part of gaming night.

Their idea was simple: create a place where gamers could quickly discover multiplayer games that fit their interests, budgets, and friend groups.

Today, SquadUp Gaming represents that mission: bringing friends together through great gaming experiences.

Our Mission

To make multiplayer PC gaming more accessible, enjoyable, and social by helping friends discover games worth playing together.
