What Platforms Do You Need to Download PC Games?

Unlike gaming consoles, PC gaming does not rely on a single digital storefront. Different publishers distribute their games through different platforms.

Fortunately, most PC gaming launchers are free to download. You only pay when purchasing games or optional subscription services.

Popular PC Gaming Platforms

1. Steam

Best Starting Point

Steam is one of the most widely used digital gaming platforms for PC. It offers a massive selection of games across nearly every genre.

Steam also includes friend lists, community discussions, game reviews, and multiplayer invitations.

Popular multiplayer games: Counter-Strike 2, Helldivers 2, and Phasmophobia.

Our recommendation: Start with Steam if you are new to PC gaming.

2. Epic Games Store

Great for Free Games

Epic Games Store is another major platform for downloading PC games. It features popular multiplayer titles and regularly offers promotional free games.

Popular multiplayer games: Fortnite and Rocket League.

Our recommendation: Check Epic Games Store regularly for free game promotions.

3. Xbox App and PC Game Pass

Subscription Option

The Xbox app allows Windows users to purchase, download, and manage supported PC games.

PC Game Pass is an optional paid subscription that provides access to a rotating library of games.

Popular multiplayer games: Minecraft and Sea of Thieves.

Our recommendation: Consider a subscription if your group enjoys trying several different games each month.

4. Battle.net

Competitive Gaming

Battle.net is Blizzard Entertainment's gaming platform. It allows players to download, update, and launch supported Blizzard games.

Popular multiplayer games: Overwatch 2 and World of Warcraft.

Our recommendation: Install Battle.net if your friends regularly play Blizzard titles.

5. EA App

Sports and Action Games

The EA app is Electronic Arts' PC gaming platform. It provides access to supported EA games, downloads, updates, and account features.

Popular multiplayer games: EA SPORTS FC and Battlefield.

Our recommendation: Use the EA app when playing supported Electronic Arts titles.

Tips for New PC Gamers

  • Start with Steam before installing every available launcher.
  • Check whether a game supports cross-platform multiplayer.
  • Compare prices before purchasing games on different storefronts.
  • Review your computer's system requirements before downloading.
  • Enable account security features such as two-factor authentication.
