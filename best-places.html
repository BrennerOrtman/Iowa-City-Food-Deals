Where Should You Eat in Iowa City?

Iowa City offers a wide variety of dining options, from casual pizza shops to traditional diners and Mediterranean restaurants.

Whether you're a University of Iowa student or a local resident, these real restaurants offer different dining experiences.

The following selections are editorial recommendations rather than an official restaurant ranking.

Mediterranean Food

1. Oasis Falafel

Location: 206 N Linn Street, Iowa City

Oasis Falafel is a local Mediterranean restaurant known for falafel, hummus, pita sandwiches, and other Middle Eastern-inspired dishes.

The restaurant is a good option for diners interested in vegetarian-friendly meals and Mediterranean flavors.

Best For: Mediterranean Food

Breakfast & Diner Food

2. Hamburg Inn No. 2

Location: 214 N Linn Street, Iowa City

Hamburg Inn No. 2 is a longtime Iowa City diner serving traditional American comfort food.

The restaurant is known for breakfast dishes, burgers, sandwiches, and classic diner meals.

Its history in Iowa City makes it a recognizable part of the local restaurant scene.

Best For: Breakfast and Comfort Food

Pizza

3. Mesa Pizza

Location: 114 E Washington Street, Iowa City

Mesa Pizza is a downtown pizza restaurant offering individual slices and specialty pizzas.

Its menu features creative topping combinations alongside traditional pizza options.

Pizza by the slice makes it a convenient choice for a quick meal.

Best For: Pizza by the Slice

American Dining

4. Pullman Bar & Diner

Location: 17 S Dubuque Street, Iowa City

Pullman Bar & Diner offers American-style dining with a menu featuring burgers, breakfast dishes, and other diner favorites.

Its sit-down atmosphere makes it an option for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

Best For: Casual Sit-Down Dining

Coffee & Mediterranean Food

5. Cortado Mediterranean Cafe

Location: Iowa City, Iowa

Cortado Mediterranean Cafe serves coffee, Mediterranean-inspired food, and affordable lunch options.

Its menu includes pita wraps, falafel dishes, and other casual meals.

A published menu listing included an original falafel pita wrap for $6.95.

Best For: Coffee and Affordable Lunch

Barbecue

6. Pop's BBQ

Location: Iowa City, Iowa

Pop's BBQ specializes in barbecue dishes, including smoked meats, sandwiches, and rotating menu features.

Its menu includes options such as pulled pork and smoked chicken sandwiches.

A published menu listing included a pulled pork sandwich for $8.29.

Best For: Barbecue and Sandwiches
