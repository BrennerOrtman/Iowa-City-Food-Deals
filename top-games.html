What Are the Best Multiplayer PC Games Right Now?

Finding the perfect multiplayer game can be challenging when everyone in your friend group enjoys something different.

Our fictional SquadUp editorial team selected six standout multiplayer games based on their gameplay variety, teamwork opportunities, replay value, and overall appeal for friend groups.

These are editorial picks rather than a live ranking of review scores. Game popularity and player opinions can change over time.

Our Top Multiplayer PC Game Picks

1. Helldivers 2

Cooperative Action

Helldivers 2 challenges players to work together during intense missions against dangerous alien enemies.

Teamwork, communication, and chaotic combat make it a memorable cooperative experience.

Players: Up to 4
Genre: Third-Person Shooter
Best For: Cooperative Missions

2. Minecraft

Creative Survival

Minecraft gives friends the freedom to explore, build, survive, and create their own adventures together.

Whether building a massive city or surviving the night, players can create experiences that fit their group's interests.

Players: Varies by Server
Genre: Sandbox Survival
Best For: Creative Groups

3. Phasmophobia

Cooperative Horror

Phasmophobia puts players in the role of paranormal investigators searching haunted locations.

Friends must gather evidence, communicate effectively, and survive frightening supernatural encounters.

Players: Up to 4
Genre: Horror
Best For: Scary Game Nights

4. Lethal Company

Comedy and Horror

Lethal Company sends players into dangerous environments to collect valuable scrap while avoiding monsters.

Its combination of unpredictable situations and cooperative gameplay creates hilarious moments with friends.

Players: Up to 4 in Standard Play
Genre: Cooperative Horror
Best For: Chaotic Friend Groups

5. Counter-Strike 2

Competitive Shooter

Counter-Strike 2 is a tactical first-person shooter focused on precision and teamwork.

Players coordinate strategies, manage resources, and compete in intense matches against opposing teams.

Players: 5 vs. 5 Competitive
Genre: Tactical Shooter
Best For: Competitive Groups

6. Stardew Valley

Relaxed Cooperative Gaming

Stardew Valley allows friends to manage a farm, explore caves, fish, and participate in community activities.

Its relaxed pace makes it a great alternative to fast-paced competitive games.

Players: Multiple Players
Genre: Farming Simulation
Best For: Casual Gaming Nights

Which Game Should Your Group Choose?

If your friends enjoy competitive matches, try Counter-Strike 2. For teamwork and action, consider Helldivers 2.

If your group prefers creativity and exploration, Minecraft provides endless possibilities. For a more relaxed experience, Stardew Valley is a strong option.
